THE STORY

Welcome to the island where nothing is as it seems.

The year is 2045. A passenger plane crashes on a remote island hidden from the outside world. Alex, the lone survivor, must find a way to survive.

The island is filled with dinosaurs, mysterious creatures, abandoned facilities, and the remains of experiments that were never meant to be discovered.

Deep inside a secret laboratory lies the truth about PROJECT ZERO. Every discovery brings Alex closer to understanding what happened on this island.

Explore, gather resources, tame creatures, and prepare to face the island's greatest threat.
