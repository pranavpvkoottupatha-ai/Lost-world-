THE STORY

The year is 2045. After a passenger plane crashes on a mysterious island, Alex must find a way to survive in a dangerous, unexplored world.

Dinosaurs roam the island. Abandoned laboratories hide secrets. Strange creatures reveal traces of an experiment known as Project Zero.

Explore the island, gather resources and prepare for the final battle. Alex's survival depends on courage, exploration and smart decisions.
