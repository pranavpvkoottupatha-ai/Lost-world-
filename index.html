THE STORY

Welcome to the island where nothing is as it seems.

The year is 2045. A passenger plane crashes on a remote island hidden from the outside world. Alex, the lone survivor, must find a way to survive.

The island is alive with dinosaurs, dangerous creatures, abandoned facilities, and the remains of experiments that were never meant to be discovered.

Deep inside a mysterious laboratory lies the secret of PROJECT ZERO. As Alex explores the island, the truth behind the experiments begins to emerge.

Explore, survive, tame creatures, discover hidden locations, and prepare to face the island's greatest threat. Every discovery brings Alex closer to the truth.
