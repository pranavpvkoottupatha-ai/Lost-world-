THE STORY

The year is 2045. After a passenger plane crashes on a mysterious island, Alex must find a way to survive in a dangerous, unexplored world.

Dinosaurs roam the island. Abandoned laboratories hide secrets. Strange creatures reveal traces of a mysterious experiment known as Project Zero.

Explore the island, uncover the truth, collect resources and prepare for the final battle. Alex's survival depends on courage, exploration and smart decisions.
