THE STORY

Survival is only the beginning.

The year is 2045. After a passenger plane crashes on a mysterious island, Alex must find a way to survive among dangerous dinosaurs and genetically engineered creatures.


Hidden laboratories, abandoned research facilities and strange experiments reveal clues about a secret project. Explore the island and uncover the truth behind PROJECT ZERO.
